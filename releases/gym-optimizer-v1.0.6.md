# SakaLuX Gym Optimizer v1.0.6

- Aec3 renamed to **SakaLuX**, prioritizing DEX with editable weights (DEX 1.25, Speed 1, Strength 0.8, Defense 0).
- Previous Aec3 users migrate automatically to the SakaLuX preset.
- More compact inline Train Next, Headroom and Stats UI, with color bars.
- Avoid unsupported recommendations when stat measurements are insufficient.

Notes: DEX weights are SakaLuX-specific planning preferences, not verified Aec3 mechanics. Unlock detection, real headroom formulas and exact StatForge feature parity remain unimplemented. TornPDA visual QA pending.
